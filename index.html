Take the right medicine at the right time.

A simple and smart medicine reminder that helps you track your daily doses without stress.

The Problem

Many people miss or mistime their medicines. This can make treatment more difficult, especially for elderly and long-term patients.

How MediMind Helps

⏰ Smart Schedule

Keep track of your medicine timings easily.

🔔 Instant Alerts

Get reminders so you don't forget your dose.

✓ Track Doses

Take, skip or undo your medicine status.

🤖 Ask AI

Get simple general guidance for common questions.

Our Design Thinking Journey

1. Empathize

We understood the struggles people face with medicines.

2. Define

We identified the need for simple and timely reminders.

3. Ideate

We selected a phone-friendly web app as our solution.

4. Prototype

We built MediMind using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

5. Test

We test reminders, tracking and ease of use.
